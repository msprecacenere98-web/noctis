# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Food enthusiasts and discerning critics approaching the site deliberately: they want to understand the philosophy before deciding. They arrive in the evening, in a research mindset, from a recommendation or a social reference. They are willing to spend time — the site is itself a test of the experience. Secondary audience: guests returning to book, who already know what they want.

## Product Purpose

NOCTIS is a fine dining restaurant combining Japanese precision with Western restraint. The site exists to build desire and generate reservations in equal measure. Neither takes priority: the experience must seduce first so that the conversion feels earned, not forced. Success is a guest who arrives with expectation already formed.

## Brand Personality

Silenzioso, materico, raffinato. Silent weight. The brand does not announce itself — it is simply present. Every element earns its place or disappears. The tone is unhurried; confidence comes from control, not volume.

## Anti-references

- Classic Italian fine dining: no white marble, no gold serif on cream, no candlelight clichés, no soft-focus tablecloth photography.
- The visual language of NOCTIS has nothing to do with Mediterranean luxury or trattoria heritage. The references are closer to Japanese architecture and Scandinavian material culture than to anything Italian.

## Design Principles

1. **Restraint communicates.** Silence and negative space say more than any ornament. Emptiness is not absence — it is the room breathing.
2. **Material over decoration.** Texture, weight, and the physical quality of surfaces matter more than graphic effects. Every visual element should feel like it has mass.
3. **Seduce before converting.** Desire must exist before the ask. The reservation CTA appears once the guest is already persuaded, not before.
4. **Precision as craft.** Every typographic decision, every gap, every transition timing is intentional. Carelessness is visible at this level of refinement.
5. **The experience starts at load.** The site is the first moment of the NOCTIS experience. It must feel like arriving.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio for body text. Meaningful alt text on all images. Visible focus states. Reduced-motion support via `prefers-reduced-motion` for all scroll and entrance animations.
